Getting in

Tell us in advance and the practical things are arranged before you arrive rather than improvised at the door.

Access, honestly described.

Step-free

Belváros and Andrássy are fully step-free with lift access from street level. Buda has three steps at the entrance and a portable ramp — call ahead and it will be waiting.

Wheelchair transfer

Every studio has at least one room with space for a chair-side transfer, and two treatment rooms at Belváros take a wheelchair directly without transferring at all.

Hearing

Induction loops at all three receptions. Our clinicians will drop the mask to speak if you lip-read — just say so at the start rather than halfway through.

Quiet appointments

First slot of the day at any studio means an empty waiting room, no drilling audible from other rooms, and as long as you need in the chair.

Children

The Buda children's room has its own entrance off the courtyard, so nobody under ten walks past adult treatment on the way in.

Languages

Hungarian and English at every reception. For German, French, Italian or Slovak, request the clinician when booking and we will match the slot.

Out of hours

Something has broken and it is Sunday.

The emergency line is answered by a clinician, not a call centre, every evening and all weekend. Most problems are managed over the phone until Monday; when they are not, one of us opens Belváros.
