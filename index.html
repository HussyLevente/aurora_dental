01 — The practice

Founded by Dr. Nóra Kelemen in a two-chair room above a bookshop on Váci utca. Three studios later, the rules have not changed.

We built the practice we could never find as patients.

Most dental anxiety is not about pain. It is about not knowing — what is happening, what it will cost, whether you are being sold something. So we removed the unknowns one at a time until what was left felt calm.

Appointments here run about a third longer than the industry average. Every plan is written down and priced before a single instrument comes out. And the ceramics lab is thirty steps from the chair, so the person making your crown can see your face.
