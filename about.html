Seventeen years of doing less, on purpose.

Aurora began in 2009 as two chairs in a room above a bookshop. Three studios and twenty-four thousand patients later, the founding rule has never been rewritten: the least intervention that solves the problem is the right one.

01 — Why any of this exists
“I spent eight years in practices where the appointment was fifteen minutes and the price appeared at the end. I did not want to work that way, and I certainly did not want to be treated that way.”

The practice was designed backwards from that sentence. Longer slots so nobody feels rushed. Written quotes so nobody feels ambushed. A rule that the first visit treats nothing, so nobody is talked into anything while numb and half-reclined.

It is not a business model anyone would recommend on a spreadsheet. It turns out to be an extremely good one anyway: sixty-one percent of the people who walk through the door were sent by somebody who already had.

02 — What we hold to

06 — Working here

We hire slowly and keep people a long time.

Aurora has lost two clinicians in seventeen years. That is partly the longer appointments — it is a considerably better way to spend a working day — and partly that nobody here has a treatment target, because we have never set one.

We are usually looking for hygienists and a dental nurse or two. If you have read this far and recognised something, write to us even when nothing is advertised.
